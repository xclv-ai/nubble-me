# The consumer — who Nubble is for (draft for the owner to correct, 2026-10-14)

Sources: the owner's own words (marked **O**), the research file `docs/redo/01-BEST-PRACTICES-2026-10-13.md` section C (NN/g, HIG cognitive accessibility — marked **R**). Everything else is **my assumption (A)** and is the part to correct.

## The niche, in one line
A founder of an early-stage AI startup who is neurodivergent (ADHD-like attention), who has to know what moved in their market today and what to do about it — and who will not read long articles to find out. **O**

## Profile — "Maya, solo founder" (illustrative, not a real person)
- **Role:** founder / CEO of a 1–10 person AI startup, pre-seed to Series A; does product, sales and marketing at once. **A**
- **Product:** one product with a URL (e.g. pokpok.ai); a handful of named competitors. **O** (product URL), **A** (the rest)
- **Brain:** "we don't have patience" **O**; attention drops fast on long text; reads headlines and numbers, skips paragraphs **O**; time-blindness and task-switching cost **R** (C1, C6, C7); needs an end ("happy sense of completion") **R** (C5, C10).
- **Day:** checks the phone between calls; 2–3 minutes per session **A** (Wordle-length, **R** C10); morning and evening.
- **What they read today:** X/LinkedIn feeds, newsletters, Hacker News, TechCrunch — too much, mostly irrelevant; no tool tells them "this one is about YOU". **A**
- **Taste:** strange, internet-native, likes humour and personality (Rick and Morty register) **O**; bored by corporate dashboards **O** ("generic and boring"); wants something memorable, game-like **O**.

## Needs, ranked (owner's four first, in his order)
1. **No long text — infographics, facts, metrics.** **O** A number, a diagram, a before/after beats a paragraph.
2. **Red alert when a competitor posts something important.** **O** Immediate, unmissable, only when it matters.
3. **Clear WHAT TO DO with this news.** **O** One concrete action per signal, already written; accept or skip. **R** C3
4. **Only what matters to MY product.** **O** ("you see everything which will help you with your product") — relevance to the product decides what appears.
5. Depth on demand, never forced: glance first, more only if they swipe. **O** (Nubble's 2-axis concept)
6. Finish fast and stop: a set that ends. **R** C5, C10
7. Big readable type, high contrast, one decision per screen. **O** ("large and readable"), **R** C2, C4, C8, C9
8. Cheap to run. **O** ("cost-efficient").

## What they will hate (each one already happened on this project)
- Walls of text, small grey type, fixed headers eating the screen (failures N09, N18, N20).
- Buttons instead of the swipe (N19).
- Pretty pictures that say nothing about the news (N16).
- No way out of a screen (N10).
- Generic dashboard look (N17).

## Success, in their terms (A — to agree with the owner)
- Opens the app, knows in **5 seconds** whether anything in their market moved.
- Code red seen within the hour, with the competitor's name and the one fact.
- Finishes today's signals in **under 3 minutes**, with 1–3 actions in their Reminders.
- Comes back tomorrow because it was fast, useful and funny.
