# Open questions — answer before the next design (2026-10-14)

Ordered by how much the answer changes what gets built. Owner's answers go under each line.

## Blocking (the design cannot be right without these)
1. **One news item = what on screen?** One infographic card per story (same template every time), or the visual form chosen per story type (a launch → before/after; funding → number; competitor move → you vs them; event → calendar)?
2. **What does swiping left/right (depth) go through now?** Proposed: 0 glance (headline + one big fact) → 1 facts (numbers, timeline) → 2 your product (impact, threat/opportunity, you vs them) → 3 what to do. Is there any long text at all, even at the deepest level?
3. **Home: a list or a daily round?** All of today's signals in one vertical list you scroll (Nubble's vertical axis), or the 3-a-day round that ends ("discharged")?
4. **Code red:** a competitor = only names on your watchlist, or any company in your market? Push notification, or red on the home only?
5. **Where facts come from:** the RSS text has almost no numbers. May the app read the full article page (free on the phone, or a paid reader like Firecrawl), so the cloud step can extract real numbers?

## Shapes the look
6. **Dr HAOS on screen:** visible character (the pink cat) on the home, or his voice only — one roast line per signal?
7. **Physical objects + open wires:** on the home only, or also on the reading pages?
8. **Images per news (paid, Seedream):** wanted, and at what daily budget — or infographics built from facts only (free)?
9. **3D + gyroscope:** home only, or every screen?

## Scope
10. One product (pokpok.ai) or several products per user?
11. "Useful" after reading = which action: a Reminder, a note, share to Slack, a draft post?
12. First users: you only, or TestFlight testers (then the gateway key question: strip it, or key + $5 cap)?
