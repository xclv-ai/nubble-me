# What is a nubble?

**A nubble is a source-linked unit of information that connects what happened to what matters for your focus and what you might do next.**

**Nubble** is the method that creates and presents those units. **To nubble** is to turn available content into a relevant, inspectable account of its possible consequences for a particular decision. These are proposed meanings for this project, not dictionary definitions.

## The shortest explanation

> **Nubble makes information earn your attention.**

## The ten-second explanation

> Nubble connects the original story to what matters for your focus, then explains what could change and what you could do next. You can inspect the evidence and decide whether to act.

## The three questions

| Representation | Question | Example output |
|---|---|---|
| Full | What does the source say? | The available original, with its date, source, and retrieval limits. |
| Short | What matters to my focus? | The supported facts selected for my current goal and decision. |
| So what? | What could this change for me? | A conditional implication and a feasible next step, verification, monitoring rule, or reason to keep the current plan. |

A nubble keeps those three representations connected. Its value depends on the reader's context. The same source can justify a verification task for one reader and no action for another.

## Why the name works

The English word **nub** includes the sense of the central point or gist. That gives the name a useful association with finding the point that matters. [Merriam-Webster, “nub,” sense 3](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/nub).

The project's existing naming notes already explored this association. The current proposal extends it from the essential point of a text to the essential point **for a reader's decision**. This is a brand interpretation; it does not claim a verified historical derivation of the project name.

There is no need to turn NUBBLE into an acronym. An invented expansion would add a phrase to remember without explaining the experience. The name also does not need claims about ancient-language origins, a trademark's availability, or a guaranteed reduction in reading time.

## A compact example

**Source:** a competitor pilots weekly AI brand mention reports.

**Short, for a hypothetical positioning decision:** the feature overlaps with part of your message; the announcement does not establish full feature parity.

**So what?** verify the overlapping capability before changing comparison copy. Keep the roadmap unchanged on this evidence alone.

The [complete fictional example](examples/competitor-update.md) supplies the source and contrasting reader contexts. It illustrates intended behavior; it is not a model-performance result.

## Wording for different audiences

**Repository description:**

> Full → Short → So what? A research framework connecting source evidence, personal relevance, and proposed next steps under limited attention.

**Research paper definition:**

> We define a nubble as a source-linked representation bundle containing an available original, a context-conditioned summary, and separately labeled conditional implications and decision options. The framework permits appropriate inaction and abstention.

**Product introduction:**

> Tell Nubble what you are focused on. Read what matters, inspect why it matters, and consider the next step with the source one click away.

**Launch line:**

> Attention is currency. Information should earn it.

## Language rules

- Say **could change** when a consequence is inferred rather than observed.
- Say **proposed next step** until a person accepts an action.
- Use **no action warranted** when evidence supports keeping the plan, and **insufficient evidence** when the system cannot justify a recommendation.
- Keep dates, source scope, and uncertainty visible when they affect a decision.
- Treat the phrase “decision-ready” as an ambition to test, rather than a blanket reliability guarantee.
- Describe effectiveness with study evidence when it exists. The current release is a methods proposal with a prototype reference.
