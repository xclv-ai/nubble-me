# Focused related-work evidence for Nubble

Research date: 25 October 2026. This is a targeted primary-source investigation, not an exhaustive or systematic review. The purpose is to identify the closest precedents and changes that would make a Nubble methods paper testable and credible. Nine selected records and the search queries are in [related-work-sources.json](related-work-sources.json). “Full text read” means relevant primary methods/evaluation/limitation passages were inspected, not a claim of cover-to-cover review.

## Main conclusion

The broad idea “personalize a summary, explain why it matters, and propose an action” is already present in prior work. Nubble should not claim to invent personalized summarization, decision-focused summarization, intermediate user representations, reader-specific utility, or summary-to-recommendation pipelines.

The strongest potentially defensible contribution is a **specified and evaluated linked representation**: available original evidence; a summary selected for a particular user's current goal and constraints; and a clearly distinguished interpretation giving conditional implications plus a feasible act, verify, monitor or no-action option. Its value must be demonstrated against strong alternatives under the same total attention budget. That is a proposed contribution to test, not an established novelty finding.

## Closest studies and what they change

| Study | Verified overlap and boundary | Consequence for Nubble |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hsu & Tan, **Decision-Focused Summarization**, EMNLP 2021 | DecSum selects sentences to preserve a supervised model's decision, represent the sentence-level decision distribution and reduce redundancy. The primary human protocol tests future restaurant-rating choices from summaries. This is a direct precedent for evaluating summary usefulness through decisions. | Cite prominently; do not present “moving from text relevance to decisions” as new. Distinguish factual evidence, model-decision faithfulness and human decision quality. [Official record](https://aclanthology.org/2021.emnlp-main.10/), [method](https://arxiv.org/html/2109.06896v1). |
| Zhang et al., **PersonalSum**, NeurIPS 2024 | Personalized news summaries, user profiles and source-sentence alignments are collected together. User interests include plot and structure as well as entities/topics. This is a direct precedent for user-specific content and original-source linkage. | Use real goals and prior knowledge rather than assuming an occupation or keyword list captures the reader. Preferred content is not evidence of improved action choice. [Proceedings](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2024/hash/b3c318cd7ee132d8a6b1895a2d6436c7-Abstract-Datasets_and_Benchmarks_Track.html), [primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.03905v1). |
| Ghodratnama & Zakershahrak, **SumRecom**, arXiv 2024 | Active concept-preference feedback and reinforcement learning personalize summaries. It explicitly considers elicitation burden and evaluates with 75-word summaries and a small preference study. | Count onboarding and feedback as attention costs. A preference win is not a decision-outcome win. Other publication status not verified. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.07294), [method](https://arxiv.org/html/2408.07294v1). |
| Spinoso-Di Piano & Cheung, **Does This Summary Answer My Question?**, arXiv 2024 | Uses a reader model to rerank query-focused summaries by answer reconstruction, tested on news, meetings and stories. Authors explicitly acknowledge limitations of overlap metrics and absence of extensive human validation. | Reader-aware generation is prior art. An LLM reader simulation can be an auxiliary check, not the primary evidence that a human is better informed. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.06524), [experiments](https://arxiv.org/html/2411.06524v1). |
| Zhang & Chen, **Personalized Chain-of-Thought Summarization of Financial News for Investor Decision Support**, 2025 | A four-stage system processes original financial news into initial summaries, enhanced summaries, then keyword-conditioned relevance, implications and exactly three investment recommendations. This is the closest direct functional overlap with “summary + So What.” | An ordinary staged prompting baseline is mandatory. Nubble's possible advance must lie in auditability, richer constraints, conditionality, warranted abstention and measured utility. Recommendation outcomes are not established by their summary/retrieval metrics. ArXiv comments claim ICDM Workshops; independent proceedings metadata was not verified. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.05508), [stages and limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.05508v2). |
| Li & Chaturvedi, **Comparative Personalization for Multi-document Summarization**, arXiv 2025 | ComPSum creates a structured user analysis from authored texts compared with same-topic texts by others. AuthorMap tests personalization through authorship attribution; PerMSum covers news and reviews. | Structured user analysis is prior art. Distinguish style/identity recognition from satisfying a current goal. The authors' profile definition and comparable-document requirement limit transfer to ordinary news readers. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.21562), [method/limitations](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.21562v1). |
| Pu, Gao & Wan, **Is Summary Useful or Not?**, LREC-COLING 2024 | Measures human QA, classification and similarity judgments and completion time using originals versus summaries. The inspected preprint finds substantial time savings but worse QA from system summaries. | Report both time and errors. Sample evaluation questions from source evidence and tasks, not from whatever the summary happens to include. An intrinsically good summary can fail a specific information need. [Proceedings](https://aclanthology.org/2024.lrec-main.821/), [preprint protocol and results](https://arxiv.org/html/2305.15044v1). |
| Kirstein et al., **Re-FRAME the Meeting Summarization SCOPE**, arXiv 2025 | FRAME uses statement–context facts; SCOPE considers reader knowledge, projects, goals, urgency, relevance and uncertainty before selection. P-MESA measures several reader-fit dimensions and is calibrated to human annotations. | A context-aware fact-selection pipeline is a strong comparator. Reader-fit scoring remains a proxy for correct prospective choices. Inspecting a model's generated explanation does not prove it has the user's true context. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15901), [primary method and nine questions](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15901v2). |
| Cachola et al., **Information Satisfaction**, 2026 | Tests persona-aware informational usefulness, perturbations and reader-owned preferences. Human study uses genuine queries, self-reported expertise/use cases, identical retrieval context and blinded comparisons. The paper finds poor agreement between reader preferences and automatic metrics, including persona-conditioned judges. | This is the most direct warning against claiming validity from an LLM rubric. Add audience-swap and meaning-preserving length perturbations; validate against the actual reader and independent task performance. [ACM full text](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3834580.3838749), [arXiv version](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.14457v1). |

None of these sources establishes Nubble's effectiveness. Conversely, a feature not found in the passages inspected is not proof that it has never existed.

## Concrete method upgrades

1. **Make the user context an input with a schema.** At minimum, record current decision/question, role and relevant expertise, known information, time horizon, available actions, authority/resources and constraints. Mark missing context. A user should be able to correct it. Separate relatively stable profile information from a time-specific decision.
2. **Separate evidence from interpretation in the output contract.** Give factual propositions source-span identifiers and publication dates; preserve qualifications, disagreement and material risks. Link each relevance explanation to the particular user-context field that makes it relevant. Label inferred implications as conditional reasoning, not as facts quoted from the source.
3. **Define the “So What” unit precisely.** A useful unit has an evidence basis, a context premise, a conditional implication, uncertainty or counterevidence, and a next-step class. Action options require preconditions and feasible scope. “Verify” identifies the unresolved proposition and a discriminating check. “Monitor” names an observable trigger and review horizon. “No action” explains why the user's current decision does not warrant intervention. Do not force a recommendation count.
4. **Make abstention a real decision.** Distinguish irrelevance, insufficient evidence, infeasible action, and adequate evidence for maintaining the current course. Evaluate false action and false dismissal separately. “No action” must not be a catch-all for model uncertainty.
5. **Treat the attention budget as a measured constraint.** Include onboarding, reading all visible layers, opening originals, verification, feedback and follow-up. Report both generated length and actual elapsed task time. A longer three-layer output can be less efficient even if every section is individually useful.
6. **Specify transformations and failure checks.** Preserve a machine-readable relation from each implication/action to source claims and user premises. A second pass should detect unsupported implications, omitted material caveats, contradictory claims, invented context and infeasible actions. Report how frequently the checks fail, not just that checks exist.

A narrow contribution statement that survives these precedents would be:

> We specify a source-linked representation that separates user-context relevance from conditional decision implications, and test whether making those links explicit improves feasible decisions and calibrated deferral under a fixed total attention budget.

This remains a research hypothesis until comparison and ablation results exist.

## Evaluation that can support the claim

Use a prospective or time-stamped replay task with a frozen evidence bundle and explicit user context. Prevent future information from leaking into input, expected answers or hindsight labels. Include irrelevant news, conflicting evidence, tempting but infeasible recommendations and cases where verification, monitoring or no action is best. Ground expert adjudication in the case's available information; permit several justified actions when appropriate.

Minimum comparators:

- Available original evidence with equivalent navigation and the same task-time limit.
- A strong length-controlled generic summary.
- A strong personalized/task-focused summary given exactly the same user context.
- A single-pass context-to-recommendation prompt and an ordinary staged personalized-summary-to-implications prompt, with the same model and total output budget.
- Nubble with explicit links and action/deferral choices.

The cleanest representation ablation holds the substantive wording and length constant while removing the visible links or separating layers. A separate ablation can remove user constraints, conditionality or no-action options. Otherwise a win could come merely from extra tokens, a better prompt, more context or more model calls.

Preregister one primary behavioral endpoint, such as expert-adjudicated feasible action choice or decision utility in cases with a defined action set. Report task accuracy/error severity and time separately; plot their frontier across at least two budgets if feasible. Secondary outcomes can include material-risk recognition, calibrated confidence, appropriate verification/monitoring, source-check success, cognitive effort and reader preference. Keep source fidelity, user relevance, inference warrant, feasibility and preference as separate measures.

Use counterbalanced within-person designs only with different but matched cases to prevent memory effects. Randomize condition order and blind evaluators to method. Analyze participants and cases as repeated units; give uncertainty intervals and the planned minimal effect of interest. A pilot should estimate variance and usability, then inform a real sample-size calculation. Do not invent a target participant count as proof of rigor.

LLM judges can support annotation triage. Calibrate them against human labels for each construct, test audience and constraint swaps, and inspect disagreements. Do not use the same model to create outputs, generate ground truth and certify utility without independent human validation.

## Citation and evidence cautions

- Correct DecSum citation: **Chao-Chun Hsu and Chenhao Tan, EMNLP 2021, 117–132, DOI 10.18653/v1/2021.emnlp-main.10**. A later financial-evaluation paper misstates the first author's name and cites .47, which is Text2Mol.
- PersonalSum has a verified NeurIPS publication. The financial CoT paper's workshop label is author-reported in arXiv comments. SumRecom, RSA, ComPSum and FRAME should retain preprint wording unless another publication is verified.
- The 2026 Information Satisfaction DOI resolves to matching ACM full text, but its exact conference label was not established in the passages/metadata inspected. Cite the verified DOI or arXiv record without guessing the venue.
- The Pu et al. detailed results inspected are from the 2023 preprint; its matching 2024 proceedings metadata and abstract were checked. Preserve that version distinction for numerical results.
- Promising historical task-oriented meeting-summary studies (2008–2009) were discovered but not full-text verified. They are recorded as follow-up candidates, not empirical support.
- A narrow financial market-decision paper was read but omitted from the core comparison: only three human investors and a narrow short-horizon setup, plus citation errors, make it inappropriate to use as strong corroboration.
- No autonomous agent research jobs or full-site crawls were launched. Targeted search, canonical metadata and relevant primary passages were used. Shared Firecrawl request limits briefly throttled retrieval; the central close precedents and methodological alternatives were nevertheless resolved.
